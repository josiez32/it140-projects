## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Escape the Haunted Mansion — a spooky adventure game where the player is trapped inside a mysterious haunted mansion and must explore the rooms to find magical items and escape.

**Storyline:**

The player wakes up trapped inside a haunted mansion and must explore the mansion and collect seven magical items: an Ancient Book, Silver Key, Golden Candle, Magic Mirror, Enchanted Ring, Ruby, and Crystal Skull. The player must collect all of the items before entering the Secret Chamber, where the Shadow Master is waiting. If the player enters the Secret Chamber before collecting all of the items, the Shadow Master catches the player and the game ends.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Foyer — Start room
2. Library
3. Kitchen
4. Study
5. Bedroom
6. Ballroom
7. Basement
8. Trophy Room
9. Secret Chamber — Villain room

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items. Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Ancient Book — Library
2. Silver Key — Kitchen
3. Golden Candle — Study
4. Magic Mirror — Bedroom
5. Enchanted Ring — Ballroom
6. Crystal Skull — Basement
7. Ruby — Trophy Room

## Villain

The villain is the Shadow Master, a mysterious supernatural figure who controls the haunted mansion. The player must avoid the Secret Chamber until all seven required items have been collected.
